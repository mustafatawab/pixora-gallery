### Feature 1 — Private client galleries
Highest priority
Photographers create a gallery for each client or event, then share a password-protected link. Clients can view photos without creating accounts.
- Favorites and shortlist selection
- Download permissions and expiry dates
- Watermarks and private access
- ZIP downloads and original-quality files
Business value: A concrete problem solved for working photographers.




### Feature 2 — Portfolio website builder

Let every photographer create a public page such as yourdomain.com/username, with an optional custom domain on paid plans.
- Portfolio themes and customizable layouts
- About, services, contact, and booking pages
- Featured projects and categorized albums
- SEO metadata and social sharing previews
Business value: Makes the product useful every day, not just when uploading photos.




### Feature 3 — Client and business management

Create a lightweight workspace for professional photographers.
- Client directory and shoot history
- Booking requests and shoot dates
- Delivery status and client feedback
- Basic invoices, payment status, and follow-up reminders
Business value: Creates recurring value beyond image hosting.




### Feature 4 — Creator analytics

Show photographers which portfolios and galleries attract attention.
- Portfolio visits and referral sources
- Most-viewed projects
- Gallery views and download activity
- Inquiry conversion tracking
Business value: Gives paying users evidence of engagement and helps them improve their marketing.




### Feature 5 — AI photography assistant

Add AI only where it reduces real work.
- Generate titles, captions, and image descriptions
- Suggest tags and searchable categories
- Find visually similar photos
- Help clients locate their photos in large event galleries
Business value: Faster organization and a differentiated premium feature.

Start with automated captions and tagging. More complex visual search can come later.
